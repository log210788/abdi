## 13–28 min | Core New Skill: ✅ `I` vs ❌ `I don't` + `Do you...?`
*Use **Tab 1 (`I / I don't`)** and **Tab 2 (`Do you...?`)** in `yes_no_routines.html`.*

### Step A: Visual Rule (30 seconds max!)
Show green ✅ and red ❌ on screen:
- 🟢 **YES:** `"I watch my phone."` / `"I drink coffee."` / `"I eat at home."`
- 🔴 **NO:** `"I **don't** watch TV."` / `"I **don't** eat out."` / `"I **don't** have a TV."`
- *(Tip for Lewis: Do NOT explain "auxiliary verb". Just point to 🔴 **don't = NO**.)*

### Step B: Live Picture Choice (6 Easy Verbs Only!)
Lewis shows one picture at a time and asks ONE short question. Abdi answers **for himself** (real life!):
1. **☕ Coffee** (`drink_coffee.svg`): *"Do you drink coffee in the morning?"* ➔ *"Yes, I do. I drink coffee."* (or *"No, I don't."*)
2. **📺 TV** (`no_tv.svg`): *"Do you watch TV?"* ➔ *"No, I don't. I **don't** watch TV."*
3. **📱 Phone** (`watch_phone.svg`): *"Do you watch your phone?"* ➔ *"Yes, I do. I watch my phone."*
4. **🍽️ Eat out** (`eat_out.svg`): *"Do you eat out every day?"* ➔ *"No, I don't. I eat at home."*
5. **🧺 Market** (`go_market.svg`): *"Do you go to the market on Friday?"* ➔ *"Yes, I do. I go on Friday."*
6. **⚽ Football** (`play_football.svg`): *"Do you play football on Saturday?"* ➔ *"Yes, I do."* / *"No, I don't."*

### Step C: Quick Role Flip (2 mins — Boosts Confidence!)
Let Abdi ask **Lewis** 2 questions using the frame on screen:
- *"Lewis, do you watch TV?"*
- *"Lewis, do you drink coffee?"*
*(Lewis answers in 1 short sentence: "No, I don't!" / "Yes, I do!")*

## 28–38 min | Gentle Bridge to Lesson 4: `He / She` + `doesn't`
*Use **Tab 3 (`He / She`)** in `yes_no_routines.html`.*

### One Tiny Visual Rule (Show, Don't Lecture!)
- 🟢 **He / She YES:** Keep the **`-s`**! ➔ `"He cook**s**."` / `"She studie**s**."` / `"He play**s** football."`
- 🔴 **He / She NO:** Use **`doesn't`** (the **`-s` goes away!**) ➔ `"He **doesn't** cook."` / `"She **doesn't** watch TV."`

### Guided Practice (About Abdi's Real Friend or Family Member):
Ask Abdi to name **one friend or brother/sister** (e.g., Dawit / Almaz / his brother). Build **4 short true sentences** together:
1. *"His name is ________."*
2. *"He / She wakes up at ________."*
3. *"On Saturday, he / she ________."* (e.g., *plays football / cooks / goes to the market*)
4. *"He / She **doesn't** ________."* (e.g., *doesn't watch TV / doesn't eat out*)
