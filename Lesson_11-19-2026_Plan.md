## 13–28 min | Core Skill 1: Places I Go & Don't Go (`I go to the...` vs `I don't go to the...`)
*Use **Tab 1 (`1. Places`)** and **Tab 2 (`2. Do you go?`)** on `index.html`.*

### Step A: Visual Pattern (30 seconds max!)
Show the 6 Place Cards on screen:
- 🟢 **YES:** `"I go to **the** coffee shop."` / `"I go to **the** park."` / `"I go to **the** gym."`
- 🔴 **NO:** `"I **don't** go to **the** cinema."` / `"I **don't** go to **the** market."`
- 🌍 **Countries & Cities Rule (From Nov 17, 23:30–25:30):**
  - Town places use **`the`**: *"I go to **the** coffee shop."* / *"I go to **the** hospital."*
  - Countries & cities have **NO `the`**: *"I go to **Japan**."* / *"I live in **Ethiopia**."* (NOT ❌ *to the Japan*)

### Step B: Live Speaking — "What About You, Abdi?" (6 Places)
Tap each card in **Tab 2 (`2. Do you go?`)** and ask Abdi for his real answer:
1. ☕ **Coffee Shop** (`coffee_shop.svg`): *"Do you go to the coffee shop?"* ➔ *"Yes, I do. I go to the coffee shop."*
2. 🌳 **Park** (`go_park.svg`): *"Do you go to the park?"* ➔ *"Yes, I do. I go to the park."* (or *"No, I don't."*)
3. 💪 **Gym** (`go_gym.svg`): *"Do you go to the gym?"* ➔ *"Yes, I do."* / *"No, I don't."*
4. 🎬 **Cinema** (`go_cinema.svg`): *"Do you go to the cinema?"* ➔ *"No, I don't. I watch my phone."*
5. 🧺 **Market** (`go_market.svg`): *"Do you go to the market on Friday?"* ➔ *"Yes, I do."* / *"No, I don't."*
6. 🇯🇵 **Japan** (`countries`): *"Do you go to Japan?"* ➔ *"No, I don't. I live in Ethiopia."*

## 28–38 min | Core Skill 2: One Friend (`Does he / she...?` & `doesn't` without `-s`)
*Use **Tab 3 (`3. Does he/she?`)** on `index.html`.*

### Step A: One Tiny Visual Rule
Point to the yellow tip box on Tab 3:
- ❓ **Question:** `"**Does** he play football?"` (NO `-s` on *play*!)
  - 🟢 **YES:** `"**Yes, he does.** He **plays** football."` (`-s` comes back in a YES sentence!)
  - 🔴 **NO:** `"**No, he doesn't.** He **doesn't** play football."` (NO `-s` with `doesn't`!)

### Step B: 5 Guided Picture Drills
Go through the 5 cards in **Tab 3**:
1. ⚽ **Football:** *"Does he play football?"* ➔ Tap 🟢 YES (*"Yes, he does. He plays football."*) and 🔴 NO (*"No, he doesn't. He doesn't play football."*)
2. ☕ **Coffee Shop:** *"Does he go to the coffee shop?"* ➔ *"Yes, he does. He goes to the coffee shop."* / *"No, he doesn't. He doesn't go to the coffee shop."*
3. 🍳 **Cook Dinner:** *"Does she cook dinner at home?"* ➔ *"Yes, she does. She cooks dinner."* / *"No, she doesn't. She doesn't cook dinner."*
4. 📚 **Study English:** *"Does she study English?"* ➔ *"Yes, she does. She studies English."* / *"No, she doesn't. She doesn't study English."*
5. 🍽️ **Eat Out:** *"Does he eat out every day?"* ➔ *"No, he doesn't. He doesn't eat out."*
