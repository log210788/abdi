## 🌟 Part 1: Warm-up & Introductions Review

### Useful Sentence Frames:
- **Names:**
  - "My name is **[Name]**."
  - "His name is **[Name]**." (for a boy/man)
  - "Her name is **[Name]**." (for a girl/woman)
- **Age:**
  - Question: *"How old are you?"*
  - Answer: *"I am **[age]** years old."*
- **Origin:**
  - Question: *"Where are you from?"*
  - Answer: *"I am from **[Country/City]**."*

## 📦 Part 2: Prepositions of Place (in, on, under, next to)

| Preposition | Meaning / Clue | Example Sentence |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **IN** | Inside a box, bag, room, or container | The pen is **in** the pencil case. 🖊️ 🎒 |
| **ON** | Touching the top surface of a table, desk, wall, or floor | The laptop is **on** the desk. 💻 🪵 |
| **UNDER** | Below or beneath something | The shoes are **under** the chair. 👟 🪑 |
| **NEXT TO** | Beside / right by the side of something | The coffee cup is **next to** the laptop. ☕ 💻 |
