# Lesson Plan — Abdi
**Date:** Monday, November 16, 2026 (50 mins)  
**Goal / Context:** A1 confidence-building in **daily routines, telling time (*quarter past / half past / quarter to*), prepositions (*at / in / on*), and 3rd-person `-s/-es`** (*he/she goes, watches, cooks, studies*).  
**Speaking Ratio:** **70% Student / 30% Lewis** | **Teacher Story Cap:** Max **60 seconds** (use short, clear questions and give Abdi plenty of time to build full sentences!).

---

## 00–05 min | Warm-Up & Bridge from Last Lesson
- **Friendly Check-In:** How are you today, Abdi? What day is it today, and what time is it right now in Ethiopia?
- **Quick 3-Question Routine Warm-Up:**
  1. What time did you wake up this morning? (*"I woke up at..."*)
  2. Did you eat breakfast **at home** today?
  3. What do you do **on Friday**? (*"On Friday, I go to the market."*)

## 15–40 min | Core Speaking Task: My Week vs. My Friend's Week
**Setup (1 min):** Abdi builds **his own real weekly routine** first (*I...*), then compares it with a friend or family member (*he / she + -s/-es*). Lewis prompts only with short questions (*"What time?"*, *"Which day?"*, *"And your friend?"*).

### Open Prompts (Guided A1 Speaking)
1. **Morning Routine (4 sentences):** Walk me through your morning from waking up to lunch. Use **at + time** and **in the morning**.
   - *Frame:* `"I wake up at ___. I wash my face and brush my teeth. I eat breakfast at ___."`
2. **Evening & Night Routine (3–4 sentences):** What do you do **in the evening** and **at night**? (Cooking, studying English, watching your phone, sleeping.)
3. **Flip to He / She (`-s / -es` Practice):** Tell me about a friend or brother/sister! How is their day different from yours?
   - *Targets:* `"He wakes up at..."` / `"She cooks on..."` / `"He goes to work at..."` / `"She watches..."` / `"He studies English on..."`
4. **Weekend Choice:** What do you do **on Saturday** and **on Sunday**? Do you eat **at home** or **eat out**?

### Target Frames to Practice
- `I wake up at [time]` / `He wakes up at [time]`
- `in the morning` / `in the evening` / `at night`
- `I go to the market on Friday` → `She goes...`
- `I watch...` → `He watches...`
- `I study...` → `She studies...`
- `eat at home` vs. `eat out`

---

## 40–50 min | Live Silent-Scribe Debrief + Micro-Homework
- **Silent-Scribe Debrief (7 min):** Praise **2 things Abdi did well** (e.g., remembering *at* for times, using *quarter past / half past*) + review **3 sentences** he said during the 15–40 min task, gently fixing any missing `-s/-es` or *at/in/on*. Have him read all 3 clean sentences aloud.
- **Micro-Homework (< 5 min):** Send a **30–45 second voice note** on Preply saying **4 true sentences**:
  1. `"I wake up at ___ in the morning."`
  2. `"I eat dinner at ___ in the evening."`
  3. `"On Friday, I go to ___."`
  4. `"My friend [name] watches ___ / studies ___."`
