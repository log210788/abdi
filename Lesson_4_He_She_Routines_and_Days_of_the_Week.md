## 📅 Part 2: Target Vocabulary 1 — Days of the Week & Weekly Time Expressions

In English, the days of the week always start with a **CAPITAL letter** (e.g. *Monday*, not *monday*).

| Day of the Week | IPA Pronunciation | Ethiopian (Amharic 🇪🇹) | Af-Soomaali (🇸🇴) | Everyday Example Sentence |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Monday** | /ˈmʌn.deɪ/ | **ሰኞ** (*Segno*) | *Isniin* | I go to English class on **Monday**. |
| **Tuesday** | /ˈtjuːz.deɪ/ | **ማክሰኞ** (*Maksenyo*) | *Talaado* | He studies on **Tuesday**. |
| **Wednesday** | /ˈwenz.deɪ/ *(silent 'd')* | **ረቡዕ** (*Rebu*) | *Arbaco* | She works at the clinic on **Wednesday**. |
| **Thursday** | /ˈθɜːz.deɪ/ | **ሐሙስ** (*Hamus*) | *Khamiis* | We meet friends on **Thursday**. |
| **Friday** | /ˈfraɪ.deɪ/ | **ዓርብ** (*Arb*) | *Jimce* | I pray and visit family on **Friday**. 🕌 |
| **Saturday** | /ˈsæt.ə.deɪ/ | **ቅዳሜ** (*Qidame*) | *Sabti* | He plays football on **Saturday**. ⚽ |
| **Sunday** | /ˈsʌn.deɪ/ | **እሑድ** (*Ihud*) | *Axad* | They relax with family on **Sunday**. |

### Useful Weekly Time Expressions:
- 🏢 **On weekdays** (/ˈwiːk.deɪz/) ➔ 🇪🇹 *የስራ ቀናት* / 🇸🇴 *Maalmaha shaqada* (Monday to Friday)  
  *Example:* "I study English **on weekdays**."
- 🏖️ **At the weekend** / **On the weekend** (/ˈwiːk.end/) ➔ 🇪🇹 *ቅዳሜና እሁድ* / 🇸🇴 *Dhammaadka todobaadka*  
  *Example:* "I relax and visit friends **at the weekend**."
- 🔄 **Every day** (/ˈev.ri deɪ/) ➔ 🇪🇹 *በየቀኑ* / 🇸🇴 *Maalin kasta*  
  *Example:* "I brush my teeth **every day**."
- 🗓️ **Once a week** (/wʌns ə wiːk/) ➔ 🇪🇹 *በሳምንት አንድ ጊዜ* / 🇸🇴 *Toddobaadkiiba mar*  
  *Example:* "I go to the market **once a week**."

## 🏃‍♀️ Part 3: The Core Grammar Rule — Third-Person Singular (*He* / *She* / *Name*)

> [!IMPORTANT]
> When we talk about **ourselves** (*I*), we use the base verb:
> - ✅ *"**I wake up** at 7:00."*
> - ✅ *"**I eat** breakfast."*
> 
> When we talk about **one other person** (*He*, *She*, or a person's name like *Marion*, *Dawit*, *Almaz*), English verbs **MUST change their ending**! We add **-s**, **-es**, or change irregular verbs:
> - ✅ *"**He wake<u>s</u> up** at 7:00."*
> - ✅ *"**She eat<u>s</u>** injera."*
> - ✅ *"**Marion live<u>s</u>** in the city."*

---

| **1. Verbs ending in -sh, -ch, -ss, -x, -o** | Add **-es** | *wash*<br>*brush*<br>*watch*<br>*go*<br>*do* | **washes**<br>**brushes**<br>**watches**<br>**goes** (/ɡəʊz/)<br>**does** (/dʌz/) | ይታጠባል (*yitatebal*)<br>ይቦርሻል (*yiboreshal*)<br>ይመለከታል (*yimeleketal*)<br>ይሄዳል (*yihedal*)<br>ያደርጋል (*yadergal*) | *Wuu maydhaa*<br>*Wuu cadaystaa*<br>*Wuu daawadaa*<br>*Wuu tagaa*<br>*Wuu sameeyaa* |

## ⏰ Part 4: Prepositions of Time Matrix (*AT* vs *IN* vs *ON*)

| Preposition | When to Use | Examples |
| :---: | :--- | :--- |
| **AT** ⏰ | Exact clock times, meal times, and night | • **at** 7:30 AM<br>• **at** half past eight<br>• **at** noon / midday<br>• **at** night |
| **IN** ☀️ | General parts of the day, months, and years | • **in** the morning<br>• **in** the afternoon<br>• **in** the evening |
| **ON** 📅 | Days of the week, dates, and days with parts of the day | • **on** Monday<br>• **on** Friday<br>• **on** Saturday morning<br>• **on** the weekend |

### Easy Memory Formula:
- **AT** = The Clock ⏰ (*at 8:00*)
- **IN** = The Part of Day 🌅 (*in the morning*)
- **ON** = The Calendar Day 📅 (*on Monday*)

## 👩‍💼 Part 5: Spotlight Story — "Marion's Busy Daily Routine" (DK Unit 13)

Meet **Marion**. She works in an office in the city. Look at what Marion does every weekday:

```text
       06:45 ➔ Marion takes a shower.
       07:00 ➔ Marion has breakfast in the kitchen.
       07:20 ➔ Marion brushes her teeth.
       07:30 ➔ Marion leaves home.
       07:45 ➔ Marion catches the bus.
       08:30 ➔ Marion arrives at work.
       17:00 ➔ Marion leaves work and goes home.
```

### Full Story Text:
> *"Marion lives in the city. Every Monday to Friday, she **gets up** at 6:30 in the morning.  
> At 6:45, she **takes** a shower.  
> At 7:00, she **has** breakfast with a hot cup of tea.  
> She **brushes** her teeth at 7:20, and she **leaves** her house at 7:30.  
> At a quarter to eight (7:45), Marion **catches** the bus to work.  
> She **arrives** at the office at 8:30.  
> She **works** hard all day. At 5:00 in the afternoon, she **leaves** work and **goes** home.  
> In the evening, she **cooks** dinner and **watches** television."*
