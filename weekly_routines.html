Days of the Week & Weekly Habits

Tap each day to hear clear native English pronunciation and learn how to use ON!

Golden Rule: Days of the week in English always start with a capital letter: Monday, Friday, Sunday. We use preposition ON: "I study English on Monday."
Third-Person Rule

Transform "I" into "He / She"

Add -S

Most verbs: eats, works, wakes up, sleeps, cooks

Add -ES

End in -sh, -ch, -ss, -o: washes, brushes, watches, goes

Irregular!

have ➔ has | study ➔ studies
