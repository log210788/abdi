## 🌟 The Golden Grammar Rule: "When Do We Add -S or -ES?"

In English, when you talk about **yourself** (*I*), the verb stays in its **base form**.  
When you talk about **one other person** (*He*, *She*, or someone's name like *Abdi*, *Dawit*, *Almaz*, *Marion*), the verb **MUST change**!

```text
  👤 I watch TV.        ➔   👨 He watches TV.       /   👩 She watches TV.
  👤 I wash my face.    ➔   👨 He washes his face.  /   👩 She washes her face.
  👤 I drink coffee.    ➔   👨 He drinks bunna.     /   👩 She drinks bunna.
  👤 I have a book.     ➔   👨 He has a book.       /   👩 She has a book.
```

> [!IMPORTANT]
> **Summary of the 4 Spelling Rules for *He* / *She*:**
> 1. **Rule 1 (Most Verbs):** Just add **-s** (e.g. *eat ➔ eats*, *sleep ➔ sleeps*, *work ➔ works*).
> 2. **Rule 2 (Verbs ending in -ch, -sh, -ss, -x, -o):** Add **-es** (e.g. *watch ➔ watch<u>es</u>*, *wash ➔ wash<u>es</u>*, *brush ➔ brush<u>es</u>*, *go ➔ go<u>es</u>*).
> 3. **Rule 3 (Consonant + -y):** Drop the **-y** and add **-ies** (e.g. *study ➔ stud<u>ies</u>*, *tidy ➔ tid<u>ies</u>*).  
>    *(Note: Vowel + y just adds -s: play ➔ plays, buy ➔ buys).*
> 4. **Rule 4 (Irregular):** `have` becomes **`has`**!

## 🇪🇹 Ethiopian Language Quick Clue (አማርኛ / Amharic):
In Amharic, verbs also change when you talk about **He** (እሱ) vs **She** (እሷ):
- እኔ እመለከታለሁ (*Ine emeleketalehu* - I watch)
- እሱ ይመለከታል (*Isu yimeleketal* - He watches)
- እሷ ትመለከታለች (*Iswa timeleketalech* - She watches)
Just like Amharic adds **ይ-** (*yi-*) for *He* and **ት-** (*ti-*) for *She*, English adds **-s** or **-es** to the end of the verb!

---

## 👂 Pronunciation Secret for Abdi (/s/ vs /z/ vs /ɪz/)

When adding *-s* or *-es*, native English speakers make **3 different ending sounds**:
* **Sound 1: /s/** (sharp "s" like a snake 🐍) ➔ after sounds like *t, p, k, f*:  
  *eats*, *sleeps*, *works*, *speaks*.
* **Sound 2: /z/** (vibrating "buzz" like a bee 🐝) ➔ after vowels and voiced sounds:  
  *plays*, *cleans*, *lives*, *reads*, *goes*.
* **Sound 3: /ɪz/** (adds an **extra syllable** "-iz" 🔊) ➔ after *ch, sh, s, x, z*:  
  *watch-es* (2 syllables!), *wash-es* (2 syllables!), *brush-es*, *teach-es*, *fix-es*.

> • *play ➔ play**s*** (NOT ❌ *plaies*)  
> • *buy ➔ buy**s*** (NOT ❌ *buies*)
