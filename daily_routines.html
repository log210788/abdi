Teacher Lewis's Time Rule

:00 ➔ o'clock "It is 7 o'clock." Exactly on the hour.
:15 / :30 ➔ past "quarter past 7" (15 min after)
"half past 7" (30 min after)
:45 ➔ to "quarter to 8" (15 min before the next hour).
