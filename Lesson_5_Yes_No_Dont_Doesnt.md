## 🌟 Part 1: Quick Review (Say It Clean!)
Remember! For your **daily habits** (things you do every day or every week), use **`I` + verb** — **NOT** `I'm ...ing`!

| ❌ Don't say... | ✅ Say this! | 🇪🇹 Afaan Oromoo |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| ❌ *I'm eating 8:30.* | ✅ **I eat at half past eight.** | Sa'aatii 8:30tti nan nyaadha. |
| ❌ *I'm going market Friday.* | ✅ **I go to the market on Friday.** | Jimaata gabaa nan deema. |
| ❌ *Am eating in kitchen.* | ✅ **I eat at home.** | Mana keessatti nan nyaadha. |
| ❌ *I'm not look TV, I phone.* | ✅ **I don't watch TV. I watch my phone.** | TV hin ilaalu. Bilbila koo nan ilaala. |

---

## 🟢🔴 Part 2: Saying YES and NO (`I` vs `I don't`)

When you want to say **NO** about your daily life, put **`don't`** before the verb!
> **`don't`** = **do not** = **LAKKI ❌**

| Picture | 🟢 YES (`I ...`) | 🔴 NO (`I don't ...`) | 🇪🇹 Afaan Oromoo |
| :---: | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 📱📺 | I **watch** my phone. | I **don't watch** TV. | Bilbila koo nan ilaala / TV hin ilaalu |
| ☕ | I **drink** coffee. | I **don't drink** tea. | Buna nan dhuga / Shaayii hin dhugu |
| 🏠🍽️ | I **eat** at home. | I **don't eat** out. | Mana keessatti nan nyaadha / Alaatti hin nyaadhu |
| 🧺 | I **go** to the market. | I **don't go** on Monday. | Gabaa nan deema / Wiixata hin deemu |
| 🍳 | I **cook** dinner. | I **don't cook** breakfast. | Irbaata nan bilcheessa |
| 🎧 | I **listen** to music. | I **don't listen** at night. | Muuziqaa nan dhaggeeffadha |

## 👨👩 Part 4: One Friend (`He` / `She` + `doesn't`)

Remember Lesson 4 (just like Afaan Oromoo changes *nan baradha / ni barata / ni baratti*!):
- 🟢 **YES (`He` / `She`)** ➔ add **`-s` / `-es`**:
  - *"He **plays** football."* (*Inni kubbaa miilaa ni taphata.*)
  - *"She **cooks** dinner."* (*Isheen irbaata ni bilcheessiti.*)
  - *"He **watches** TV."* (*Inni TV ni ilaala.*)
- 🔴 **NO (`He` / `She`)** ➔ use **`doesn't`** (**NO `-s` on the verb!**):
  - *"He **doesn't** play football."* *(Inni hin taphatu.)*
  - *"She **doesn't** cook dinner."* *(Isheen hin bilcheessitu.)*
  - *"He **doesn't** watch TV."* *(Inni TV hin ilaalu.)*

> [!TIP]
> **Easy Trick:** **`doesn't`** already has the **`-s`**! So the next verb stays clean and simple (`watch`, `cook`, `eat`, `go`).

## ⏰ Part 5: Tiny Time & Preposition Reminder
- **AT** = Clock & Night ⏰ ➔ **at** 7:00 • **at** half past eight • **at** night • **at** home
- **IN** = Part of the day ☀️ ➔ **in** the morning • **in** the afternoon • **in** the evening
- **ON** = Day of the week 📅 ➔ **on** Tuesday • **on** Friday • **on** Saturday
