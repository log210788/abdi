## 🏙️ Part 2: Places I Go & Don't Go (`I go to the...` vs `I don't go to the...`)

When we talk about **places in town**, we say **`go to the ...`**:

| Picture | Place | 🟢 YES (`I go to the...`) | 🔴 NO (`I don't go to the...`) | 🇪🇹 Afaan Oromoo |
| :---: | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| ☕ | **the coffee shop** | I **go to the** coffee shop. | I **don't go to the** coffee shop. | Mana bunaa nan deema / hin deemu |
| 🌳 | **the park** | I **go to the** park. | I **don't go to the** park. | Paarkii nan deema / hin deemu |
| 💪 | **the gym** | I **go to the** gym. | I **don't go to the** gym. | Mana ispoortii (jimii) nan deema |
| 🧺 | **the market** | I **go to the** market. | I **don't go to the** market. | Gabaa nan deema / hin deemu |
| 🎬 | **the cinema** | I **go to the** cinema. | I **don't go to the** cinema. | Mana filmii (sinimaa) hin deemu |
| 🏥 | **the hospital** | I **go to the** hospital. | I **don't go to the** hospital. | Hospitaala nan deema / hin deemu |

> [!IMPORTANT]
> ### 🌍 What about Countries, Cities, and Home? (**NO `the`!**)
> - **Countries & Cities** ➔ Use **`go to ...`** (**NO `the`**):
>   - ✅ *"I go to **Japan**."* (❌ NOT *I go to the Japan.*)
>   - ✅ *"I go to **London**."* (❌ NOT *I go to the London.*)
>   - ✅ *"I live in **Ethiopia**."* 🇪🇹
> - **Home** ➔
>   - ✅ *"I eat **at home**."* (*Mana keessatti nan nyaadha.*)
>   - ✅ *"I go **home**."* (*Gara manaa nan deema.*)

## 👨👩 Part 4: Questions About One Friend (`Does he / she...?`)

When we ask about **one other person** (**he** / **she**), we start with **`Does`**:

| ❓ Question (`Does he/she...?`) | 🟢 YES (`Yes, he/she does.`) | 🔴 NO (`No, he/she doesn't.`) | 🇪🇹 Afaan Oromoo |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Does he play** football? | **Yes, he does.**<br>He **plays** football. | **No, he doesn't.**<br>He **doesn't play** football. | Kubbaa miilaa ni taphataa? |
| **Does he go** to the coffee shop? | **Yes, he does.**<br>He **goes** to the coffee shop. | **No, he doesn't.**<br>He **doesn't go** to the coffee shop. | Mana bunaa ni deemaa? |
| **Does she cook** dinner? | **Yes, she does.**<br>She **cooks** dinner. | **No, she doesn't.**<br>She **doesn't cook** dinner. | Irbaata ni bilcheessitii? |
| **Does she study** English? | **Yes, she does.**<br>She **studies** English. | **No, she doesn't.**<br>She **doesn't study** English. | Ingilizii ni barattii? |
| **Does he eat** out? | **Yes, he does.**<br>He **eats** out. | **No, he doesn't.**<br>He **doesn't eat** out. | Alaatti ni nyaataa? |

> [!TIP]
> **Remember the `-s` Rule!**
> - 🟢 **YES:** `He play**s**` / `She cook**s**` / `He go**es**` (Keep the **`-s`**!)
> - 🔴 **NO & QUESTIONS:** `Does he **play**?` / `He doesn't **play**.` (**`Does` / `doesn't` takes the `-s` away!**)

## ✍️ Part 5: Easy Practice Exercises

### Exercise 1: Choose the Clean Sentence
1. **A)** I go to the coffee shop.  
   **B)** I'm well in the coffee shop.
2. **A)** Yes, I don't.  
   **B)** Yes, I do.
3. **A)** He doesn't play football.  
   **B)** He doesn't plays football.
4. **A)** I go to the Japan.  
   **B)** I go to Japan.
5. **A)** I go to the market on Friday.  
   **B)** I go to the market at Friday.

### Exercise 2: Answer About YOU (`Yes, I do.` or `No, I don't.`)
1. Do you go to the coffee shop? ➔ _______________________
2. Do you go to the cinema? ➔ _______________________
3. Do you go to the park on Saturday? ➔ _______________________
4. Do you watch your phone at home? ➔ _______________________

### Exercise 3: Answer About a Friend (`Yes, he/she does.` or `No, he/she doesn't.`)
1. Does he play football? (🔴 NO) ➔ **No, he doesn't. He doesn't play football.**
2. Does she cook dinner at home? (🟢 YES) ➔ **Yes, she _______. She _______ dinner.**
3. Does he watch TV? (🔴 NO) ➔ **No, he _______. He _______ watch TV.**
4. Does she study English? (🟢 YES) ➔ **Yes, she _______. She _______ English.**

### Exercise 4: Put `to the` or `to`
*Remember: Town places = **`to the`** • Countries & cities = **`to`***
1. I go __________ coffee shop.
2. I go __________ market on Friday.
3. I go __________ Japan.
4. I go __________ park.
5. I go __________ London.
