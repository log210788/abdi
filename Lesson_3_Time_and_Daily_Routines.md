## ⏰ Part 2: Target Vocabulary 1 — Telling the Time & Parts of the Day

### 1. Asking the Time:
- **Question:** *"What time is it?"* (Af-Soomaali: *Waa imisadii?*)
- **Answer Frame:** *"It is **[time]**."* or *"It's **[time]**."*

| Digital Time | Written Form | Common Everyday Spoken Form | Af-Soomaali Meaning 🇸🇴 |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **07:00** | It's seven o'clock. | It's seven. | Waa toddobadii (7:00). |
| **07:15** | It's a quarter past seven. | It's seven fifteen. | Waa toddobada iyo shan iyo toban. |
| **07:30** | It's half past seven. | It's seven thirty. | Waa toddobada iyo badhka. |
| **07:45** | It's a quarter to eight. | It's seven forty-five. | Waa siddeeda oo rubuc ka dhiman. |
| **08:00** | It's eight o'clock. | It's eight. | Waa siddeeddii (8:00). |
| **12:00 PM** | It's midday / noon. | It's twelve o'clock. | Waa duhur / 12:00 duhurnimo. |
| **12:00 AM** | It's midnight. | It's twelve at night. | Waa habeenbadh / saqda dhexe. |

### 2. The 4 Parts of the Day:
- ☀️ **Morning** (/ˈmɔː.nɪŋ/) ➔ *Subaxnimo* (e.g. 6:00 AM – 12:00 PM)
- 🌤️ **Afternoon** (/ˌɑːf.təˈnuːn/) ➔ *Galabnimo* (e.g. 12:00 PM – 5:00 PM)
- 🌅 **Evening** (/ˈiːv.nɪŋ/) ➔ *Fiidkii* (e.g. 5:00 PM – 9:00 PM)
- 🌙 **Night** (/naɪt/) ➔ *Habeenkii* (e.g. 9:00 PM – 6:00 AM)

## 📏 Part 4: Grammar Rules — Prepositions of Time (*AT* vs *IN*) & The Present Simple

> [!IMPORTANT]
> In English, when we talk about **time**, we use specific prepositions:
> - Use **AT** for exact clock times and night:  
>   ✅ *"**at** 7 o'clock"* | ✅ *"**at** 8:30 AM"* | ✅ *"**at** midnight"* | ✅ *"**at** night"*
> - Use **IN** for general parts of the day:  
>   ✅ *"**in** the morning"* | ✅ *"**in** the afternoon"* | ✅ *"**in** the evening"*

### 1. Summary Comparison Table:
| Preposition | When to Use | Examples |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **AT** ⏰ | Exact clock time, meal times, and night | **at** 7:00, **at** half past eight, **at** lunch, **at** night |
| **IN** ☀️ | Large blocks / parts of the day | **in** the morning, **in** the afternoon, **in** the evening |
| **ON** 📅 | Days of the week (Bonus!) | **on** Monday, **on** Friday, **on** the weekend |

### 2. Present Simple First Person ("I" statements):
When talking about your normal habits and daily routine:
- Use the base verb directly with **"I"**:
  - *"I **wake up** at 7 o'clock."* (NOT ❌ *"I waking up"* or *"I wakes up"*)
  - *"I **eat** breakfast."*
  - *"I **go** to school."*
  - *"I **study** English."*
